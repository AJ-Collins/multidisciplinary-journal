A Message from the Editor-in-Chief

Take a look at the editorial insights and research directions incorporated in this issue.

Editorial Note

The Editorial Board of the Multidisciplinary Journal of Technical University of Mombasa (MJTUM) is pleased to release its 5th Volume, Issue 1. MJTUM is continuously becoming a trusted open-access journal that is on the right path towards indexing.

Our 5th Volume, Issue 1 starts with a total of 4 exciting articles with more manuscripts in the process of being finalized for publication in the same issue. The 4 articles are in the domain of electrical and electronic engineering, mechanical and automotive engineering, computing, and architecture and built environment.

The content of these 4 articles is as a result of dedicated research works from the authors and co-authors. MJTUM is hereby providing a platform to share contents of original research and review works in contributing to knowledge dissemination to the scientific community and to all students in their quest to advance knowledge in their respective fields.

MJTUM will continue to provide a platform for dissemination of high quality scholarly articles. It is my sincere hope that you will enjoy reading these published articles and those to be published in this current issue as we prepare you for the release of Issue 2 of Volume 5 before the end of this year 2026.

Prof. Cosmas Munga

Editor-in-Chief, MJTUM
