Electrical & Electronic Engineering

Soiling Loss Quantification of Roof-Top Mounted Solar Module in Mombasa Using Digital Imagery Techniques

Bornvince O. Odhiambo, Eric Jobunga, Sameer Kamrudin Bachani, Gideon Kidegho

Accumulation of dust and particulate matter on photovoltaic panels significantly impacts performance efficiency in coastal climates. This study introduces a digital imagery quantification framework tailored for rooftop solar modules operating in Mombasa, offering automated maintenance scheduling based on surface opacity analysis.
DOI: 10.48039/ek1sj324.01

Mechanical & Automotive Engineering

Optimization of Tilt, List, and Skew of Sea-to-Shore Gantry Cranes

Martin N. Mae, Michael J. Saulo, Michael D. Odhiambo, Daniel N. Wang’ombe

Operational efficiency in container terminal handling heavily relies on precise gantry crane positioning. This paper presents a multi-axis control algorithm optimizing tilt, list, and skew motions during container transfer under fluctuating marine environmental conditions.
DOI: 10.48039/ek1sj324.02
PDF Download
Computing & Information Technology

A Hybrid Quantum Annealing Algorithm for Solving a Constraint-Based University Timetabling Problem

Kevin Tole, Edward Fondo, Fullgence Mwakondo

University timetabling presents NP-hard combinatorial challenges. We formulate a novel hybrid quantum annealing framework that significantly accelerates convergence rates compared to conventional genetic algorithms under heavy resource constraints.
DOI: 10.48039/ek1sj324.03
PDF Download
Architecture & Built Environment

Integrating Quantitative Rigour and Contextual Insight: A Mixed-Methods Paradigm for Strategy-Environment-Performance Research in Emerging Real Estate Markets

Erastus Mwanjama Katani, Jackson Kivui Maalu

Emerging real estate sectors demand research methodologies combining statistical robustness with local market nuances. This paper synthesizes empirical paradigms to evaluate strategic performance within rapidly evolving urban African economies.
